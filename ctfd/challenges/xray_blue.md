# X-Ray Blue - Lightweight Defense

This is an educational cybersecurity exercise, not a medical diagnostic tool.

## Interactive Red/Blue Arena

An instructor either pairs this CTFd account with a Red account or enables solo mode so one account can play both roles. Open the Blue notebook from your own workspace:

[Open the Blue Team notebook](/workspace-launch?notebook=xray_blue/blue_team.ipynb).

The validated Red image appears at the top of the notebook when you open it from this challenge page. Inspect it beside the clean source, then experiment locally with randomized smoothing. Submit bounded parameters (`sigma` from 0 to 0.1, 10–500 noisy views, and an abstention threshold from 0 to 1). The protected evaluator independently checks clean utility and the defended prediction. A successful defense returns your X-Ray Blue flag for submission here. Red can see your current parameters and adapt after your response.
